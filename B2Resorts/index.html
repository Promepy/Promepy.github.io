How do I book a stay?+

Call +91 99665 53200 or WhatsApp the resort with your dates and guest count. The team will confirm room availability, rates and booking terms directly.

How close is the beach?+

The resort is on Ramapuram Beach Road in Chirala, within walking distance of Ramapuram Beach.

Is there a swimming pool?+

Yes, B2 Resorts has an outdoor swimming pool. Ask the team about pool timings, access and any guidelines for your stay.

What time are check-in and check-out?+

Listed check-in is 11:00 AM and check-out is 10:00 AM. Please confirm early arrival or late departure arrangements directly with the team.

Can I enquire about a group or special occasion?+

Yes. Share your preferred dates, group size and occasion with the resort on WhatsApp to discuss the available options and arrangements.
