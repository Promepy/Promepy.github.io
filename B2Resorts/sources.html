Google reviews

The two selected, short excerpts shown on the home page are from Google reviews by Manoj Patel and sare navaneeth, each displayed with five stars on the public Google search listing. The wording and reviewer names have been preserved. These are selected positive excerpts, not a complete representation of all feedback. The full Google listing includes mixed feedback.

The aggregate rating is a dated snapshot, not a live feed. “Read all Google reviews” opens the original Google review page. This website does not impersonate a live Google reviews widget.

Photography

All resort, room, pool, beach and food photographs used on this concept were downloaded from the public B2 Resorts Google photo gallery. The hero image was shown under the resort owner’s “B2 Resorts” attribution. Other images may have been contributed by guests. Images remain the property of their respective creators. This concept claims no ownership of them.

Local files are used so the gallery does not depend on expiring Google image links. The source URLs are recorded in the photo manifest. Room photographs illustrate the property; they do not guarantee a particular room category or view. Request current category-specific photographs from the resort before booking.

Videos

Four public Google gallery clips are hosted locally and play in the gallery viewer with standard video controls. They do not autoplay. The clips show a resort walk-through, the pool, the rain-dance area and beach waves. Attribution and original source URLs are in the video manifest.

Privacy

The enquiry form stores no data and submits nothing to this website. It opens WhatsApp with the chosen dates, guest count and room preference in a draft message. You decide whether to send it. The Google Maps embed makes requests to Google and is subject to its privacy policy. Instagram receives a request only if you follow a post or profile link. This concept includes no analytics or tracking added by its creator.
